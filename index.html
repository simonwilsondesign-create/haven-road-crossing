About the gauge, forecast & status

The gauge is BOM station 040813, Upper Brookfield TM on Moggill Creek. Gauge height is not water depth over the road. The 1.49 m unsafe threshold and crossing location were supplied by the owner; this is not an official BOM flood class. Below the threshold does not confirm road reopening.

Gauge readings are retrieved from BOM public FTP products and may be delayed, unavailable or operationally unverified. The rain outlook comes from Open-Meteo at the crossing coordinates. It is weather context, not a river-height or reopening forecast.
